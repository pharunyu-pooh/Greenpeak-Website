GeoAsia Initiative

Building Bridges Between Thailand, Georgia, Central Asia, and South Asia

The GeoAsia Initiative defines an international collaboration established with Casus Group LLC, Georgia. We are in partnership with private sector organizations and government stakeholders, with the initiative itself supported by the Department of International Trade Promotion (DITP), Ministry of Commerce, the Royal Thai Government – Ankara and Chennai Office. The fundamental aim concerns sectors such as trade, investment, tourism, and economic cooperation across emerging markets, prioritizing the use of eco-friendly resources in facilitating them.

Our Mission

We aim to strengthen cultural connectivity intercontinentally for businesses seeking regional expansion. This currently includes Thailand, Georgia, Central Asia, the Caucasus, and South Asia.

Our Vision

By positioning Thailand as the gateway to ASEAN while also establishing Georgia as the strategic gateway to the Caucasus and Central Asia, we actively create a trade corridor that enables businesses to access new markets efficiently.

What We Do

To achieve our goals, we strive to establish our presence in a variety of important developmental areas:

International trade and investment partnerships
Market entry and business matching programs
Distribution and retail network development
Cross-border logistics and supply chain solutions
Tourism promotion and business delegation programs
Government-to-business (G2B) & B2B collaboration
Product localization and regional market expansion

Globalization with Greenpeak

The initiative works closely with government agencies each step of the way. Chambers of commerce, retailers, manufacturers, investors, and logistics partners work to contribute to successful globalised distribution.

Here at Greenpeak, we are committed to building a meaningful relationship between Thailand and Sri Lanka, helping Thai brands establish a strong market presence through trusted retail partnerships, efficient distribution networks, and carefully tailored local market strategies that create lasting value for businesses and consumers alike.
